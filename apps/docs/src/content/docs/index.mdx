## Talk is cheap. Collateral isn't.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A promise" icon="pencil">
    A team states something a program can check onchain: a deployment, an untouched vesting account, revenue into a
    treasury. With a deadline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collateral" icon="seti:lock">
    They lock funds behind it. Miss the deadline and the collateral goes to the people who bet against them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A market" icon="random">
    Anyone can buy YES or NO. The price reads as the market's odds that the team delivers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A verdict" icon="approve-check-circle">
    At the deadline the program reads chain state. No oracle votes, no disputes. Ship or slash.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
