## The mark

A rounded square with a **W** cut out in negative space. It is the whole logo: there is no separate wordmark file.
When you need the name next to it, set **windle** in Unbounded, always lowercase.

<BrandMarks />

### Clear space

Keep empty space around the mark of at least **a quarter of its width** on every side. Nothing (text, edges, other
logos) goes inside it.

### Do

- Use the blue mark on black or deep navy, the white mark on blue or busy backgrounds, the black mark on white.
- Scale it proportionally.
- Write the name in lowercase: **windle**, also at the start of a sentence.

### Don't

- Recolour the mark beyond blue, white and black, or fill it with gradients.
- Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows and effects to it.
- Put it on backgrounds that make the cut-out W hard to read.
- Imply a partnership. windle is built on Solana and works with pump.fun tokens; it is not affiliated with either.

## Colour

Dark first: a black page, a deep navy family, one electric blue accent. Text and emphasis stay black and white; blue
is used on purpose for interactive states, the market bar and glow.

## Typography

| Use | Typeface | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display, headings | **Unbounded** | Uppercase for big statements ("SHIP OR SLASH."), tight letter spacing. |
| Body, UI | **Inter Tight** | Everything you read at length. |
| Margin notes | **Caveat** | Handwritten trader-style notes on the landing page only. |

All three are open-source fonts (SIL Open Font License) and available from Google Fonts and Fontsource.

## Voice

Short, declarative, a little dry. "Talk is cheap. Collateral isn't." We describe what the protocol does and what it
doesn't, we don't promise returns, and we say "not decided yet" when it isn't.
