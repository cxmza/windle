Using windle, as a trader or as a team, can lose you money. Read this before you put funds into any market or lock any
collateral.

## No advice, no promise of returns

Nothing on windle.fun, docs.windle.fun or any windle channel is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Prices
in windle markets are opinions of other traders, not forecasts by windle. windle does not promise any return.

## You can lose everything in a position

- A share on the losing side of a market is worth **zero**.
- A team whose promise is slashed **loses its collateral**.
- Prices can move sharply, especially in thin markets and near deadlines. You may not be able to exit at the price
  you see.

## Protocol and software risk

- **Smart contract bugs.** The windle programs may contain bugs that lock, misdirect or lose funds. No audit has
  been completed. An audit reduces this risk; it doesn't remove it.
- **Mis-specified conditions.** A promise resolves on its onchain condition, which may not match how you read its
  headline. Read the condition.
- **Design changes.** windle is pre-launch. Mechanics, parameters and fees may change.

## Blockchain risk

- **Network congestion or outages** on Solana can delay trades, resolution or claims.
- **Transactions are irreversible.** Funds sent to a wrong address, or approved in a malicious transaction, are
  generally not recoverable.
- **Wallet security is yours.** windle never holds your private key and can't recover it. Anyone with your seed phrase
  controls your funds.

## Market and counterparty risk

- **Team behaviour.** The team is the party best placed to influence its own outcome. The design aims to make
  delivering the team's best strategy, but no mechanism removes this risk completely.
- **Asset risk.** If collateral or payouts are in a token whose price falls, what you receive may be worth less than
  expected.
- **Manipulation.** Thin markets can be pushed by a few traders. A price is not a guarantee.

## Legal and regulatory risk

Prediction markets and related activities are regulated, restricted or prohibited in many jurisdictions. Rules can
change and may limit or end windle's availability where you live. You are responsible for complying with the laws
that apply to you and for any taxes due.

## Scams

windle will never ask for your seed phrase, never require a transaction to sign in, and has not announced any token.
Verify links: windle's sites are on `windle.fun` and its subdomains.
