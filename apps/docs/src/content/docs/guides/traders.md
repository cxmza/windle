## The planned flow

1. **Sign in** with a Solana wallet. See [Sign in](/guides/sign-in/).
2. **Open a promise** and read its **condition**, not just the headline. The condition is what resolves the market.
3. **Back the team** by buying YES, or **bet against** them by buying NO. Your wallet asks you to approve a transaction
   showing what you pay.
4. **Hold or exit.** Until resolution you can sell your shares back to the market at the current price.
5. **Claim.** After resolution, winning shares pay 1 unit each; on a slash, NO holders also share the collateral. See
   [payouts](/concepts/payouts/).

## Checklist before you trade

- **Read the condition and the accounts.** Is it really checkable onchain? Can it flip after the deadline?
- **Look at the collateral.** How much does the team lose if it misses, and in what asset?
- **Look at the liquidity.** In a thin market, your own trade moves the price. You pay the average price across your
  trade, not the price you saw. See [Market & pricing](/concepts/market/).
- **Size it as money you can lose.** A position on the wrong side goes to zero.

## What windle will never ask for

- Your seed phrase or private key.
- A transaction to "verify" your wallet or to sign in. Sign-in is a text message, never a transaction.
- Funds sent to an address someone gives you in a direct message.

Read the [risk disclosure](/legal/risk/) before trading.
